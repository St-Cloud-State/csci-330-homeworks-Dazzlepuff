I → i E S I′
I′ → e S | ε
E → G E′
E′ → o G E′ | ε
G → x | y | z | w
S → s | d L b
L → s L′
L′ → s L′ | ε

ε denotes the empty string

i → Uniquely determines I → i E S I′
e → Only appears in I′ → e S
o → Only appears in E′ → o G E′
x, y, z, w → Uniquely determine G → x | y | z | w
s → Could be from S → s or L → s L′, but L always follows d, so there's no ambiguity.
d → Uniquely determines S → d L b
b → Only appears at the end of S → d L b
